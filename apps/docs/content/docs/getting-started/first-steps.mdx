Welcome to OpenAthlete! This guide will help you get started with the platform.

## Create Your Account

1. Navigate to the [OpenAthlete login page](https://app.openathlete.org/auth/login)
2. Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
3. Fill in your details and verify your email

## Connect Your Strava Account

OpenAthlete integrates with Strava to automatically import your workouts.

1. Go to [**Settings** → **Connectors**](https://app.openathlete.org/dashboard/settings)
2. Click **Connect Strava**
3. Authorize OpenAthlete to access your Strava data
4. Your workouts will start syncing automatically

When you also allow OpenAthlete to edit your activities, it adds an analysis to the description of each new Strava activity: the weather, the time heat, hills and altitude cost you, your normalized pace, your intensity, your personal bests and a few comparisons (a share of Everest, of a marathon). Your own text stays first. Choose its language, preview it on your latest activity, or turn it off under **Settings** → **Connectors** → **Strava**. Accounts connected before this feature start with it off: turning it on asks Strava for the edit permission.

## Import Activity Files

Activities recorded elsewhere can be imported from their FIT, GPX or TCX files:

1. Go to [**Settings** → **Connectors**](https://app.openathlete.org/dashboard/settings)
2. Under **Import activities (FIT, GPX or TCX)**, select one or several `.fit`, `.gpx` or `.tcx` files (up to 20 MB each)
3. For GPX and TCX files, choose the sport if the file does not state it
4. Click **Import**

Each activity appears in your calendar on its original date and is processed like
a synced one. Importing the same file again does not duplicate it, and a file whose
start time matches an existing activity is refused.

- **FIT** keeps all the data the device recorded (heart rate, power, laps...) and its
  summary. Planned workouts, courses and multisport files are not accepted.
- **GPX** has no laps or summary: distance, moving time and elevation gain are
  calculated from the track. Points need times, so planned routes are not accepted.
  The activity takes the track name stored in the file.
- **TCX** keeps its laps and the device's lap summaries (timer time, distance, heart
  rate). What the laps lack is calculated from the track, as for GPX. TCX only names
  running and biking, so choose the sport for anything else. Courses, workouts and
  multisport files are not accepted. The activity takes the notes stored in the file.

The same imports are available to scripts at `POST /activity-import/fit`,
`POST /activity-import/gpx` and `POST /activity-import/tcx` (see the
[API reference](/docs/api)).

## Explore Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard with:

- **Recent Workouts** - Your latest activities
- **Statistics** - Weekly/monthly summaries
- **Training Plan** - Your current training schedule
- **Progress Charts** - Visualize your improvement

## Set Up Heart-Rate Zones

Workouts and training load use your zones. To create them:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Training zones** → **Heart Rate**
2. Click **Create zones** (or **Edit zones**)
3. Choose how to enter them:
   - **Manual**: limits in bpm
   - **% of heart-rate reserve** (Karvonen): needs your maximum and resting heart rate.
     Target HR = resting HR + percentage × (maximum HR − resting HR)
   - **% of Max Heart Rate**: needs your maximum heart rate
4. Click **Save**

New zones start from six default ranges (Zone 0 below 50%, then 50–60% up to
90–100%), which you can edit. The heart rates saved in your profile are filled in
for you; enter your resting heart rate, not the lowest one of the day.

Limits are rounded to whole bpm, and a shared limit belongs to the higher zone.
Zones are saved in bpm: they do not change by themselves when your maximum or
resting heart rate changes. Open the editor again to recalculate them.

## Create a Manual Training Session

You can also add training sessions manually:

1. Right click on a day in the calendar
2. Select **Create Training**
3. Fill in the workout details:
   - Activity type (Run, Ride, Swim, etc.)
   - Date and time
   - Duration
   - Distance (if applicable)
   - Workout description...
4. Click **Create**

## View Your Progress

Navigate to **Statistics** to see:

- Weekly/monthly summaries
- Distance and time trends
- Elevation profiles
- Pace analysis
