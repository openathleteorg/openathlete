---
title: Schedule workouts on Apple Watch
description: Send planned runs and rides from your OpenAthlete calendar to Apple's Workout app.
---

On a supported iPhone running iOS 17 or later, you can schedule structured
running and cycling sessions for your paired Apple Watch. OpenAthlete reads
your own athlete calendar for today and the next seven days, using the local
dates on your iPhone. Pool swims are currently skipped.

## Enable scheduling

1. In the OpenAthlete iPhone app, open **Settings > Connectors > Apple Watch workouts**.
2. Tap **Enable scheduling** and allow OpenAthlete to schedule workouts when Apple asks.
3. On your Apple Watch, open **Workout** and look for the session on its planned day. Start it there to follow its steps.

![Apple Watch workouts settings with the Enable scheduling button](/images/apple-watch-workouts.png)

## After you change a session

Edit, move, or cancel the workout in OpenAthlete. Return to the iPhone app
to refresh the schedule, or tap **Refresh** in **Apple Watch workouts**.
**Last updated** shows when the iPhone last refreshed successfully. Check
the Workout app on your watch to confirm the session arrived.

If a session is missing, check its date and the skipped sessions in settings.
OpenAthlete shows why it skipped a workout, such as a target that Workout
cannot represent. If scheduling permission is off, open the **Watch** app on
your iPhone and select **Workout**, or open **Settings > Workout** on your
watch. Allow OpenAthlete to add workouts, then reopen **Apple Watch workouts**
in OpenAthlete.

Choose **Turn off** to stop scheduling and remove upcoming OpenAthlete
entries. Workouts you already recorded stay in your history. If Apple has
yet to confirm a removal, the settings screen shows the pending cleanup.
