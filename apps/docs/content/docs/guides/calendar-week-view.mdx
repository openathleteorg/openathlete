---
title: Weekly calendar view
description: Review a single training week on desktop and mobile.
---

Choose **Week** in the calendar to focus on Monday through Sunday.
Use the previous/next arrows or **Current week** to navigate. The selection
is remembered in this browser; a calendar URL with `?view=week` opens the
weekly view directly.

Desktop shows seven day columns. Mobile stacks the days vertically.
Session names wrap so long workout titles remain readable. Event dialogs,
context menus, templates, cycle displays and drag-and-drop work as in the
month view.

The summary below the days puts what was done next to what was planned:
duration, distance, elevation and training load, the sessions done, missed
and still to do, and the form at the end of the week. See
[Done against planned](/docs/guides/calendar-compliance). It respects the
calendar's sport filter.

The **…** menu next to the week's title copies, cuts, pastes, shifts or clears
the whole week: see [Plan whole weeks](/docs/guides/calendar-week-actions).
