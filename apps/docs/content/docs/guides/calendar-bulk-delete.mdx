---
title: Delete multiple planned workouts
description: Select uncompleted training sessions on a coach calendar to delete, move or copy them.
---

In the coach space, open an athlete's calendar and choose **Select workouts**.
Select the sessions, then choose **Delete selected**. Review the names and dates
in the confirmation dialog before deleting.

Only uncompleted training sessions can be selected. Completed activities,
sessions linked to completed activities, competitions and notes are excluded.
Changing athlete or month clears selection. Filtering hides and deselects
excluded sessions.

Requests run one at a time through the existing event deletion API, including its
authorization and provider export cleanup. If some deletions fail, the calendar
reports how many succeeded and keeps the failed sessions selected for retry.
The batch is not atomic: successful deletions are not rolled back.
Canceling the confirmation does not delete anything.

**Move** shifts the selected sessions one day or one week, earlier or later,
and **Copy** copies them to the next day or the next week. Both run as one
request, with **Undo** in their toast.

On mobile, the selection toolbar stays visible while scrolling.
