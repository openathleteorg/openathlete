## Season view

Choose **Season** (or press **S**) to see 6 or 12 months at once, one row per
week:

- **Cycles** as vertical bars. Drag down the Cycles column to create a cycle
  over those weeks; drag the top or bottom edge of a cycle to move its start
  or end. Click a cycle to open it.
- **Races**, with their priority (A, B, C).
- **Duration** and **load**, done / planned.
- **TSB**, the form at the end of the week, in italics while it is
  projected over the sessions still planned.

Above the rows, a chart draws the weekly load against the recommended range,
and the form week by week. The arrows move the season by a month; click a
week to open it in the week view.

## Workout profiles and display

Planned sessions with a structured workout show its profile under their
name: one bar per step, as wide as it lasts and as tall as it is hard, in
the colour of its zone.

The **Display** menu (gear icon next to the filters) chooses:

- the density of the cards: **compact** fits more sessions per day,
  **comfortable** shows more;
- what cards show: workout profile, duration, distance, elevation, load;
- what the week summary shows: duration, distance, elevation, load, form;
- the wellness and form row under each day.

These choices are saved with your account and follow you on every device.

## Wellness and form under each day

When you record them, each day shows last night's sleep, HRV, resting heart
rate and Hooper index, then the form (TSB) the day ends on, with a dot
coloured by status. The form of the coming days is projected over the
sessions still planned, in italics. Measurements come from your connected
devices or from **Metrics**; without any, the row only shows the form, and
the calendar does not even ask for wellness data.

## Import files by dropping them on a day

Drag FIT, GPX or TCX files from your computer onto a day of your calendar:
the import dialog opens with them, as in **Settings › Connectors**. Each file
is checked and imported on its own, on the date it was recorded. Coaches
cannot drop files on an athlete's calendar: imports go to your own
activities.

## Comments

Open a planned session or an activity to read and write its comments. They
are shared between the athlete and their coaches, and also appear in
**Messages**, with its email notifications. **Ctrl + Enter** (**⌘ + Enter**
on a Mac) sends.

Cards show how many comments a session has, in colour while some are unread.
Opening the session marks them read.
