## Colours on the cards

A planned session gets a coloured strip on its left once it can be judged:

| Strip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Done within 20% of the planned duration |
| Yellow | Done at 50 to 79%, or 121 to 150%, of the plan |
| Orange | Done more than 50% away from the plan |
| Red | Missed: its day is over and no activity is linked |

The duration is compared first; sessions planned only in distance are
compared on distance. A session done without a duration or distance goal is
green. Sessions still to do, and activities done without a plan, have no
strip. The tooltip of a card gives the status and the percentage.

The strip needs the activity to be linked to its session. Synced activities
are linked automatically when they match a session of the same day; otherwise
drag the activity onto its session, or see
[Link a completed activity to a session](/docs/guides/link-activity-suggestions).

## Weekly summary

Next to each week (or below it, in the week view), the summary shows:

- duration, distance and elevation, **done / planned**, with a progress bar;
- the training load done / planned against the recommended range: the
  lighter part of the bar is the load still planned;
- the sessions done, missed and still to do;
- **TSB**, the form at the end of the week (fitness minus fatigue), marked
  *projected* until the week is over.

## Planned load without AI

When no AI estimate exists for a session, its load is estimated from its
planned duration and target RPE, on your maximum and resting heart rates, in
the same unit as the load of your activities (TRIMP). Races count too. This
needs your heart rate profile in **Metrics**; a session without a planned
duration has no estimate.
