---
title: Statistics
description: Read your training by period and by sport, from volume to capacities.
---

**Statistics** reads your activities over a period, for one sport or all of
them, and can compare it with the period before or the same period last year.
Filters stay in the address of the page, so a view can be shared with a coach.

| View | What it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | How much you trained, how regularly, and what changed |
| Load | Your load and form, your time by intensity and your recovery |
| Capacities | What you can do: profile, terrain, endurance, physiology, performance |
| Trends | How your pace, heart rate and power evolve for one sport |
| Year in review | Your year in figures, which you can share as an image |

Capacities and Trends read one sport at a time, the one you practise most by
default. Each capacity states what the figures show and what they cannot
conclude. There is no score and no ranking against other athletes: the profile
compares your last 12 months with the 12 before.

Figures derived from your data are explained on the page, and in
[The science behind the statistics](/docs/guides/statistics-science).
