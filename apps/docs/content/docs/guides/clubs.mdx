---
title: Run a club
description: Create a club, bring its members in, organise groups, and plan, meet and write for a whole group at once.
---

A club gathers coaches and athletes in one place. Its coaches reach every
athlete of the club, plan for a group in one go, share a workout library and
run the weekly club sessions. Club features are free on openathlete.org, with
no limit on athletes or coaches, and the same on a self-hosted instance.

Everything below lives in **Settings › Clubs**, apart from planning and
messages, which start from the calendar and the messages page.

## Create the club

In **Settings › Clubs**, choose **Create a club**, then give it a name and,
if you like, a description. You are its owner.

Each member has one role:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything an admin can, and manage the admins. Hands the club over with **Hand over the club**, or deletes it. |
| Admin | Invite and remove members, change roles, manage groups, join links and invitations. |
| Coach | Reach every athlete of the club: calendar, statistics, metrics, notes and messages. Edit the club library and the club sessions. |
| Assistant | Reach only the athletes of the groups entrusted to them, and of their sub-groups. Uses the club library without editing it. |
| Athlete | Is coached by the club's staff. Sees the club's name and its staff, never the other athletes. |

Athletes a coach reaches through a club do not count toward the coach's own
plan. A coach and an athlete who were already linked keep their own link; the
club adds nothing on top.

## Bring the members in

There are three ways, all in the club's page; use the one that suits the
start of your season.

**Import the member list.** Under **Invitations**, choose **Import a list**,
then paste it or open the CSV your spreadsheet exports, one person per line:

```text
First name;Last name;Email;Group
Mathis;Chevallier;mathis.chevallier@example.com;Beginners
Anaïs;Lemoine;anais.lemoine@example.com;Beginners
```

Commas, semicolons and tabs all work, and the header line is optional:
without it, the column with an `@` is the email, the others are first name,
last name and group. Check the preview, pick **Athlete** or **Coach**, then
send. Each person gets a personal invitation by email (in their language when
they already have an account, in yours otherwise) and lands in the group of
their line once they accept. Up to 500 people at a time; lines without a
valid email are pointed out and skipped. The list of pending invitations lets
you resend or revoke each one.

**Share a join link.** Under **Join links**, the club has one link for
athletes and one for coaches. Post them in the club's group chat or a
newsletter. You can set an expiry date, choose the group new athletes land
in, and reset a link: the old one stops working at once.

**Give the join code.** The athletes' link comes with a short code, such as
`R9SAP7Q8`. Someone who already has an account types it in **Settings ›
Clubs › Join with a code**.

Before joining, everyone sees the club's name and its staff, so they know who
will see their training.

## Groups

Groups split the club by pace, level or discipline: *Road · Marathon*,
*Trail*, *Beginners*. Create them in the club's page under **Groups**, add
athletes, and entrust each group to its coaches. A group can hold sub-groups.
A coach can also keep groups of their own athletes, outside any club, in
**Settings › Athletes**.

Groups are what you pick everywhere else: when you plan for several athletes,
write to several athletes, filter the sidebar and the coach dashboard, or set
who a club session is for. Entrusting a group to an assistant is what gives
them its athletes.

## The club library

In the calendar, open the **Template Library** and switch it from **My
library** to the club's. Its folders and workouts are shared by the whole
staff: coaches create and edit them, assistants use them. To move one of your
own workouts there, choose **Share with a club** on it; a copy goes into the
club library.

A workout from the library uses each athlete's own zones and thresholds when
it lands in their calendar.

## Plan for a whole group

Three starting points open the same dialog:

- a workout of the library: **Plan for several athletes**;
- a week of an athlete's calendar: the week's menu, **Copy to other
  athletes**;
- a day: the day's menu, **Copy the day to other athletes**.

Pick a group or athletes, the first day, and optionally a start time; the
sessions keep the days between them. **Review** shows, for each athlete,
what they lack before anything is created: a threshold for the percentage
targets, matching zones, or other sessions already planned on those days, and
whether they are ill, injured or travelling. Then plan: each copy takes the
athlete's own zones, and every copy can be changed afterwards on its own.
**Undo** in the confirmation takes the whole operation back. Up to 60 items
for up to 200 athletes at once.

## Weekly club sessions

In the club's page, **Club sessions › New session** sets a recurring meeting:
a name, a place, a group or the whole club, a start time and duration, and
either a workout of the club library or notes for the athletes. Tick **Every
week** for a weekly session, with an optional end date.

Each session lands in the calendar of every athlete it is for, four weeks
ahead, and reaches their watch like any planned session. It keeps up with the
group: an athlete who joins gets the coming dates, one who leaves loses them
(within the hour). Athletes answer **I'm coming** or **I can't come** from
the session; the staff sees how many are coming on each date, the athletes do
not see each other's answers.

**Call off** removes one date from every calendar and sends a notification
to the athletes who had it. Changing the session updates the dates to come;
stopping it removes them. Sessions already done stay in everyone's history.

## Write to a group

On the messages page, the megaphone button writes to several athletes at
once. Pick a group or athletes, then choose:

- **One conversation per athlete**: each gets the message in their
  conversation with you and answers in private;
- **One group conversation**: everyone sees the others' names and answers.

**Send later** schedules the message. Scheduled messages are listed behind
the clock button, where you can cancel them; they go to the athletes you
still coach at that time.

## The coach dashboard

The dashboard sums up the period for all your athletes or one group:
sessions done against planned, volume, form, and the athletes to watch, those
who report pain, go quiet or have a message waiting. **Groups** puts the
groups side by side; select one to focus on it.

**Alerts** sets bounds on a metric for a group or some athletes, such as
heart rate variability below 48 or resting heart rate above 60. A value past
them shows on the dashboard and sends you a notification. **Compare with the
period before** shows how each figure moved, **Pick to compare** puts
athletes side by side, and **Columns** chooses what the table shows.

The sidebar lists every athlete you coach, with a search and a group filter,
so moving between thirty athletes stays quick.

## AI agents

An agent connected through [MCP](/docs/guides/ai-agents) can copy sessions
of one calendar to the athletes you coach with `copy_sessions_to_athletes`,
through the same checks as the dialog. It reports what each athlete lacks
and returns the operation's id, which you can undo in the app.

## Leaving, removing, deleting

- Removing a member, or a member leaving, removes the club's access to their
  training at once. Their data stays theirs.
- The owner cannot leave without handing the club over first.
  An owner who deletes their account passes the club to the longest-standing
  admin, or deletes it when there is none.
- Deleting the club removes its groups and links, and takes the dates to
  come of its sessions out of the calendars, telling the athletes. Dates
  already done stay in their history, and the members keep all their own
  data. Workouts of the club library go back to the library of the coach
  who wrote them.
- A coach who deletes their account leaves the workouts they put in a club
  library to the club, under its owner.

## On a self-hosted instance

Clubs work the same way. With sign-ups set to invitation only
(`SIGNUP_MODE=invite`, what the install script sets on a public domain), a
club's join link and its personal invitations also let a new person create
their account, then join the club. The join code needs an account first: on
such an instance, send the link instead. See
[Self-hosting](/docs/getting-started/self-hosting).
