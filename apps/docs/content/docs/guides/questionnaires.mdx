---
title: Questionnaires
description: Write questionnaires for your athletes, send them from the calendar, and follow their answers over time.
---

A coach writes questionnaires (how the athlete feels, how the week went,
anything worth asking), sends them to athletes from the calendar, once or
repeated, and reads the answers week after week or as charts. Questionnaires
are free for every coach, on openathlete.org and on a self-hosted instance.

## Write a questionnaire

Open **Questionnaires** in the coach sidebar, then **New questionnaire**.
Start from a template or from a blank page:

| Template | Questions | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hooper index | Fatigue, stress, muscle soreness and sleep quality from 1 to 7. Their sum, from 4 to 28, fills the athlete's Hooper index. | Hooper & Mackinnon, 1995 |
| Wellness (McLean) | Fatigue, sleep, soreness, stress and mood from 1 to 5, summed. | McLean et al., 2010 |
| Weekly check-in | How the week felt, perceived load, motivation, pain, and three free answers. | — |
| Recovery (TQR) | How recovered the athlete feels, from 6 to 20, before a key session. | Kenttä & Hassmén, 1998 |

The wording of the templates is ours, in your language: the published scales
give the items and their anchors.

Each question has a type:

| Type | The athlete answers with | Read as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale | Buttons, or a slider for long scales (1–5, 1–7, 1–10, 0–10, 1–20, 0–100, or your own), with a label for each end | A line over time |
| Number | A number in a unit (hours, kg…) | A line over time |
| Yes or no | Two buttons | A strip of yes and no |
| One choice, several choices | Buttons, one per option | How often each option was chosen |
| Short text, long text | A field | The answers, dated |

A question can be optional and carry a help line. **Preview** shows the
questionnaire as the athlete will see it.

**Score.** A questionnaire can add or average the scale questions you tick,
as one curve to follow. The questions of a score share one scale.

**Metrics.** A scale or number question can fill one of the athlete's metrics
(weight, sleep duration, resting heart rate, HRV, Hooper index…) on the day
of the questionnaire. A number asks in the metric's unit. When a device
already measured that metric that day, its value stays: it was measured, the
answer is an estimate.

### Editing a questionnaire already answered

Wording changes apply in place. Changing the type, the scale, the unit or
removing an option of a question already answered replaces it: its past
answers stay with the old question, and its charts start again with the new
one. Coming questionnaires take the new title.

**Archive** takes a questionnaire out of the library and removes its coming
occurrences from the calendars; the answers given stay. A questionnaire no
one answered can be deleted.

## Send it

In an athlete's calendar, right-click a day (or use its **Plan** menu), then
**Send a questionnaire**. Choose the questionnaire, when it opens, and
whether it repeats every day, every week or every two weeks, for up to 90
days. Sent for today after 8 am, it opens at once. Like a session, an
occurrence can be moved, copied, or sent to several athletes with
**Copy to athletes**.

## What the athlete sees

When a questionnaire opens, the athlete gets a notification on the mobile app
and an email, then one reminder if it is still waiting: a day later, or
halfway to the next occurrence for daily questionnaires. The emails can be
turned off in **Settings › Profile**, or with the link at their foot; the
notification still comes.

The questionnaire waits on the **Today** tab of the app, above the calendar on
a computer, and in the calendar itself. **Questionnaires** (in the sidebar, or
under **Progress** on the app) gathers every questionnaire the athlete got,
week by week or as charts, with their answers. The athlete can answer:

- from the opening until the next occurrence opens, or for a week when it
  does not repeat;
- and change their answers for 24 hours after sending them.

## Read the answers

- **One athlete**: open the athlete, then **Questionnaires**. *Week by week*
  shows each questionnaire of the week as a table (a column per day) with the
  text answers underneath. *By questionnaire* draws each question for its
  type, the score, the answer rate, and a table of every answer.
- **One questionnaire across athletes**: **Questionnaires › Answers** shows,
  for each athlete, how many they answered, the last answer and the last
  score, then each question with a line per athlete (their mean past seven
  athletes).

**Export CSV**, on both, saves the answers of the period as a spreadsheet: a
row per questionnaire sent, a column per question, numbers kept as numbers.
The export is on the web app, not on the mobile apps.

The AI features read the answers too. When you generate or change a session
with AI, the last two weeks of answers go with the request, so a tired
athlete gets an easier session. Agents connected through the
[MCP server](/docs/guides/ai-agents) read them with
`get_questionnaire_answers`. Both only read what you could read yourself.

## Who reads what

- Only the coach who wrote a questionnaire reads its answers, for the athletes
  they still coach. Sent for an athlete of a club, it is read by the club's
  coaches of that athlete too. Another coach of the athlete sees it is in the
  calendar, not what was answered.
- The answers belong to the athlete. A coach cannot delete an answered
  questionnaire; the athlete can. When a coach deletes their account, the
  questionnaires their athletes answered stay with the athletes.
- The answers are part of the athlete's data export, and the questionnaires a
  coach wrote are part of theirs.

## Self-hosting

Nothing to configure. The notifications need Firebase
(`FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`) and the emails a way to send them
(`SMTP_*` or `RESEND_API_KEY`), like the other notifications; without them, questionnaires still wait on the Today tab
and in the calendar.
