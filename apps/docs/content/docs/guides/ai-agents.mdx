---
title: AI agents (MCP)
description: Let Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or your own agent read your training and plan sessions in your calendar.
---

OpenAthlete is an [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server. An AI agent
you connect can read your profile, zones, metrics, activities, calendar and
training load, and, if you allow it, plan and adapt your sessions: build a
training block towards a race, rework the week after an illness, review what
you did against the plan.

You decide what each agent may do, and you can revoke it at any time in
**Settings › AI agents**.

## Connect an agent

The address of the server is in **Settings › AI agents**:
`https://api.openathlete.org/mcp` on openathlete.org, or
`https://<your domain>/api/mcp` on a self-hosted instance.

**Claude** (claude.ai, desktop and mobile apps): open **Settings ›
Connectors › Add custom connector**, paste the address, then **Connect**.
Claude opens OpenAthlete: sign in if needed and choose **Allow**.

**ChatGPT**: turn on developer mode in **Settings › Apps & Connectors**,
create a connector with the address and OAuth authentication, then allow it
in OpenAthlete.

**Claude Code**:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http openathlete https://api.openathlete.org/mcp
```

then type `/mcp` in Claude Code to sign in.

**Cursor, VS Code and your own agents**: agents that support OAuth sign in
like Claude does. Others take a personal token: in **Settings › AI agents**,
choose **Create a token**, pick what it may do and when it expires, and copy
it. It is shown once. Send it in the `Authorization` header:

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "openathlete": {
      "url": "https://api.openathlete.org/mcp",
      "headers": { "Authorization": "Bearer oa_pat_..." }
    }
  }
}
```

## What agents can do

With **read** access:

| Tool | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| `get_athlete_context` | Today's date and time zone, metrics, zones, fitness and form, the last 4 weeks of training, coming races, periods and injuries. Agents call it first |
| `get_calendar` | Planned sessions (done, missed or to do), races, notes, activities and periods between two dates |
| `get_session`, `get_activity` | A session's structured workout; an activity's laps and your answers to the post-activity questions |
| `get_activities`, `get_training_volume` | Training history and weekly volume per sport |
| `get_training_load` | Weekly load, done and planned, with fitness, fatigue and form projected over the planned sessions |
| `get_metrics`, `get_records` | Metric history (weight, resting HR, HRV, sleep...) and best efforts |
| `list_workout_templates`, `list_athletes` | Your workout library; for coaches, the athletes they coach |

With **write** access as well:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `plan_sessions` | Adds up to 60 trainings with structured workouts, races and notes at once; all are saved or none |
| `update_session` | Changes a session, a race or a note; for a repeated session, this one and the following |
| `move_sessions`, `copy_sessions`, `repeat_session` | Moves or copies sessions by days, repeats one every few weeks |
| `delete_sessions` | Deletes planned sessions; done sessions and recorded activities are never deleted |
| `create_period`, `update_period`, `delete_period` | Training blocks, and periods without training (travel, illness, injury) |
| `log_metrics`, `update_training_zone` | Records measurements, changes a zone after a test |

Workouts read and write in plain units: durations in seconds, paces as
`4:35` per km, your zones by id, or percentages of your VMA, FTP or maximum
heart rate. Planned workouts reach your watch like the ones you plan
yourself. Dates are in your time zone.

The server also offers three prompts that clients show as commands: **Build
a training plan**, **Adapt the plan** (illness, injury, trip, fatigue) and
**Review a week**.

### Examples

- "Build me a 12-week plan for a marathon on 14 March, 5 runs a week, with
  my long run on Sundays."
- "I've had a fever since yesterday. Adapt my next two weeks."
- "How did last week go compared to the plan? Is my form right for
  Saturday's race?"
- "My new FTP is 265 W: record it and update my power zones."

Changes show in your calendar at once: good agents tell you what they are
about to change and ask first. You can edit anything they planned in the
calendar, or ask the agent to put it back.

## Coaches

A coach's agent reaches the athletes they coach, with the same rules as the
app: it passes the athlete with each request, and `list_athletes` lists
them.

## Security and privacy

- Agents get access through OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, after you approve them on a
  page of OpenAthlete. They never see your password.
- **Read** and **write** are separate: an agent with read access is not even
  offered the tools that write.
- Tokens are stored hashed. OAuth access tokens last an hour and are renewed
  with single-use refresh tokens: a stolen refresh token used twice revokes
  the access. Personal tokens last 30, 90 or 365 days, or never expire.
- Revoking an agent or a token in **Settings › AI agents** takes effect on
  the next request.
- Agents reach your data, not your account: they cannot change your
  password, your connected devices or your subscription, nor delete your
  history.

What an agent does with your data is up to it and its provider: connect
those you trust.
