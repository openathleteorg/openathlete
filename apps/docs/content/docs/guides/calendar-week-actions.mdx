---
title: Plan whole weeks
description: Copy, cut, paste, shift or clear a week of training, and plan around trips or injuries.
---

Every week of the calendar has a **…** menu: at the top of its summary in the
month view, next to its title in the week view, and in the week header on
mobile.

- **Copy the week**, then open the menu of another week and choose **Paste
  the week here**. Sessions, races and notes are copied with their structured
  workouts, on the same weekdays and at the same local time. Copies start as
  sessions to do, without the activities or comments of the originals.
- **Cut the week** and paste it elsewhere to move it. Sessions already done
  stay with their activity.
- **Shift the sessions** moves the week's sessions one day or one week,
  earlier or later.
- **Delete the week's sessions** removes the sessions still to do, after a
  confirmation. Done sessions, races and notes stay.

Copies, moves and shifts show a toast with **Undo**: it deletes the copies or
moves the sessions back.

A copy or a move of a week is one request: either every event changes, or
none does. Coaches can do it on their athletes' calendars. Times follow the
athlete's time zone, so a 7:00 session stays at 7:00 after a daylight saving
change.

Coaches can also select sessions on an athlete's calendar and move or copy
them by a day or a week: see
[Delete multiple planned workouts](/docs/guides/calendar-bulk-delete).

## Repeated sessions

When planning a session, a race or a note, choose **Repeat**: every week or
every 2, 3 or 4 weeks, until a date (eight weeks by default, 90 days at
most). The occurrences keep the weekday, the local time and the workout, and
show a repeat icon.

Editing or deleting an occurrence asks whether the change applies to **this
session only** or to **this session and the following ones**. A new date
moves the following occurrences by the same number of days, to the same new
time. Sessions already done are never changed.

## Periods without training

Drag across days (or create a cycle) and choose a type: **Travel**,
**Illness** or **Injury** mark a period without training, drawn hatched in
grey. When such a period covers sessions still to do, the calendar lists them
and offers to:

- **move them after the period**: each moves by the length of the period,
  in the same order, with **Undo** in the toast;
- **delete them**;
- **keep them**.

Training blocks keep their colour and their own name.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** in the calendar to list them: **T** goes to today, **←** and
**→** to the previous or next month (or week), **M** and **W** switch views.
Hold **C** (or **Alt**) while dragging a session to copy it, and **E** while
clicking it to edit it directly. **Esc** leaves the selection.
